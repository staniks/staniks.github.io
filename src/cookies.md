# Cookie Policy

Last Updated: February 14, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how cookies and similar technologies are used on this Site. Cookies are small text files placed on your device to enhance your browsing experience.

## Use of Cookies

This Site does not use first-party cookies. It does not utilize tracking, marketing, or analytical cookies to monitor user behavior or build profiles.

## Third-Party Cookies

While this Site is cookie-free, please be aware that the third-party services linked on this page (such as donation platforms) may use cookies to facilitate transactions or remember user preferences once you navigate to their respective domains. We do not have control over the cookies set by these third-party providers.

## Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences. You may choose to block or delete cookies at any time; however, doing so may affect the functionality of the third-party platforms linked from this Site.
